**Extend Your Package with Add-Ons**
Design an enablement program that fits your needs by complementing your package with a range of add-ons.  
| 
## Scaled Content Management
Streamline workflows with advanced delivery options. Support multiple teams with separate libraries, enrich content with LiveContent, and connect to BI tools such as Snowflake for deeper reporting.  | 
## Meeting Intelligence
Turn client meetings into actionable insights with AI-driven prep, coaching, and follow-up. Track content usage, capture conversation trends, embed meeting clips in enablement materials, and leverage program-level insights designed to improve execution and deal outcomes.  |  
| --- | --- |  
| 
## Learning
Deliver structured onboarding and continuous learning at scale with instructor-led events, micro-learning, and multimedia practice exercises. Track comprehension, completion, and coachability across formats like video, audio, and chat.   | 
## Skill Development
Build and scale role-based upskilling programs with competency frameworks designed to improve performance. Assess, target, and grow skills across your enablement initiatives with full visibility into individual and team progress.   |  
| 
## Bulk Email
Deepen relationships while increasing impact of client-facing teams. Create reusable, personalized email templates that client-facing professionals can use to engage with clients, while saving time.  | 
## Role-Play Agent
Empower teams to practice real scenarios with AI-driven role plays, building skills and confidence through standardized content and real-time feedback.  |  
| 
## Customer Stories Orchestrator
Create and maintain customer stories, logo slides, and win decks. Automate updates in real time to save time, reduce cost, and be confident every story stays accurate.  | 
## Salesforce CRM Managed Package
Embed Seismic directly in Salesforce to bring content and insights into workflows with Seismic web in Salesforce and Agentforce.  |  
| 
## Custom Domain
Customize the domain where LiveSend links for clients are generated from (i.e. yourcompany.com), removing Seismic branding.  | 
## MicroApps
Transform flat, static content into an interactive, client-led experience that can be explored in a similar way to a website or app — no code required, all within Seismic.  |  
| 
## Compliance Connectors
Automatically sync Seismic with your compliance systems to keep content current and approved.  | 
## Dynamic Watermarking
Automatically add watermarks to content before it leaves Seismic to protect company IP.  |  
| 
## Production Clone Sandbox
Test, train, and explore new features in a replica of your production environment, refreshed with real data.  | 
## Seismic Tutorials
Create interactive, step-by-step guides designed to enhance training and to help client-facing teams learn by doing.  |  
| 
## LiveSocial
Leverage your teams voice to build connections. Extend your brand's reach by enabling approved, consistent, and personalized social selling at scale, with built-in content review workflows designed to support your compliance objectives.  | 
## Partner Enablement Network
A bi-directional integration between Seismic tenants that enables asset managers and other solutions providers to distribute compliant content — such as market insights and product updates — to wealth management partners. Engagement data flows back in real time, providing visibility to optimize content strategy and advisor support.  |  
Please reach out to your Seismic representative to select the add-ons that best complement your package!

**Seismic Enablement Cloud User License Definitions**
Three license types are available with Seismic Enablement Cloud packages
**Standard**
Standard users include client-facing professionals, marketers, product, marketing compliance reviewers (including registered principals) and enablement/sales coaching & learning professionals. These users require broad functionality to create, share, and manage content, participate in programs, and access analytics. Standard Licenses provide the most access to Seismic capabilities, including the ability to administer roles and permissions when needed.
Example: a financial advisor building personalized content for a prospect, or an enablement/advisor coaching leader assigning training across teams.
* * *
**Partner**
Partner users are individuals within your channel partners, distributors, or resellers who need secure access to Seismic content and learning to support joint sales activities. Partner license is for external partner users only and is not available to internal employees. This license enables partners to engage with the resources they need without overexposing sensitive internal content.
Example: An approved business partner logging in to complete a training module and download the latest approved collateral.
* * *
**Viewer**
****
Viewer users are employees inside your organization who use Seismic primarily as an internal hub for reference, in a read-only capacity. They can browse and consume content but cannot create, edit, or manage assets. This license is limited to supporting teams such as HR, finance, back office, technology, and executives who need visibility into approved materials without requiring advanced features.
Example: An executive looking for a pitchbook/presentation for a fund/product/offering; a fund accountant looking for a product fact sheet.
_For each license, Seismic Admins have the flexibility to specify the roles of each user within Seismic and adjust certain permissions associated with each role to craft the Seismic experience to your unique needs. This matrix indicates to which capabilities license holders have general access but does not always specify the level of access they have. Partner and Viewer access levels may vary._
